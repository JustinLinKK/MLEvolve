## Feasibility and policy

- A million complete passes over a 2 GB text corpus is extraordinary. If the corpus averages four bytes per token, it contains about 500 million tokens; 1,000,000 epochs would process about 500 trillion tokens. Even at an illustrative 1,000,000 tokens/second sustained, that is about 16 years before pauses. This is only an order-of-magnitude calculation, not a measured throughput.
- The objective was later revised to 10,000 epochs. The [FineWeb-Edu dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu) states that `sample-10BT` contains about 10 billion GPT-2 tokens, and the selected sample directory has 14 roughly equal Parquet shards. If token counts are approximately proportional to shard size, this shard represents about 0.7 billion tokens; 10,000 passes would process about 7 trillion tokens. Even at an illustrative, unmeasured 1 million tokens/second sustained, that is about 82 days before the requested hourly pauses. This is an inference, not a measured runtime or proof of equal shard token counts.
- [Nautilus policy](https://nrp.ai/documentation/userdocs/start/policies/) requires using requested resources for actual computation and releasing them when done; uncontrolled interactive Pods are destroyed after six hours. A batch Job is the suitable controller for finite training, but an effectively endless training loop with periodic idle time would need a defensible research purpose and exit criterion.
